How to play

Hold the phone to your forehead, screen facing out toward your friends — they can see the word, you can't.
Tilt down when they get it right, tilt up to skip it. On most phones this works automatically; if tilt isn't available, have a friend facing you tap ✓ / ✕ instead.
Pick one or more decks, set a round timer, and choose Solo for single rounds or Team (2 to 4 teams) to keep score across turns. Tap End game on the results screen to see who won.
Turn on Skips to limit each round to the classic 3 skips — after that, you can only mark words correct.
A faint ✕ in the top-left corner during a round ends it early and takes you to the results screen.
Tap 🔊 on the home screen to turn sound effects on or off.
